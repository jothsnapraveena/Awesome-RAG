# Code of conduct

This community welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat contributors, maintainers, learners, and people discussed in resources with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code, and evidence without attacking people.
- Offer actionable feedback and accept corrections respectfully.
- Credit others' work and respect their privacy and boundaries.
- Make room for questions and different perspectives. Explain unfamiliar concepts without belittling learners.
- Keep discussion relevant and disclose promotional interests.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, sharing private information without consent, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces managed for this project. They also apply when someone represents this project publicly.

## Reporting a concern

For an ordinary public moderation request, ask a maintainer to review the relevant thread without repeating abusive or private material. For a sensitive concern, use a private contact method explicitly published by a maintainer on their GitHub profile. This repository has not designated a separate confidential reporting address; do not assume a public issue is confidential. If no private project contact is available, use GitHub's reporting tools for conduct on GitHub.

Share only the details needed to review the concern. Do not publicly identify a reporter or retaliate against someone for reporting in good faith.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or block participants according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. They should explain moderation decisions where appropriate while protecting private information. A maintainer involved in a dispute should defer review to another uninvolved maintainer when one is available.

Anyone affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through an available private maintainer contact. Avoid escalating the dispute through repeated public posts. No guaranteed response time or dedicated moderation team is implied.
